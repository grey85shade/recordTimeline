Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 25, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the LifeTimeline mobile application (hereinafter referred to as the "Application"), developed by Bernat Castellet (hereinafter referred to as the "Service Provider").

Privacy First Philosophy: LifeTimeline is built with a privacy-by-design approach. Your personal memories, events, and photos are yours alone.

1. Local Data Storage (Your Content)

All content you create within the Application is stored locally and exclusively on your device. This includes:

  • Timeline Events: Titles, descriptions, and timestamps of your life milestones.
  • Categories: Custom labels used to organize your events.
  • Photographs: Any images you attach to events are copied to the Application's private internal storage.

The Service Provider does not have access to this data. We do not host your personal events or photos on our own servers, and we cannot see, read, or share your content.

2. Data Security & Encryption

To further protect your privacy, LifeTimeline implements high-level security measures:

  • Database Encryption: The local database is encrypted using SQLCipher (256-bit AES encryption). This ensures that even if someone gains unauthorized access to your device's files, the Application's data remains unreadable without your credentials.
  • Internal Storage: Photos are stored in the Application's private directory, which is not accessible by other apps on your device.

3. Information Collection and Use (Third-Party Services)

The Application utilizes third-party services that may collect information used to identify the device and improve the service. These services are provided by Google and are governed by their own privacy policies.

A. Google Analytics for Firebase

We use this service to understand how the Application is used and to optimize performance. The data collected is anonymized and technical in nature, including:

  • Session Metrics: Frequency of app launches and total duration of usage.
  • Screen Engagement: Statistics on which screens (e.g., Calendar, Timeline) are visited most frequently.
  • General Geographic Data: Country and city-level location (estimated via IP address, while adhering to Firebase protocols that obscure the exact address).

B. Firebase Crashlytics

To ensure the Application is stable and reliable, we use Crashlytics to receive technical reports when a failure occurs. This data is strictly diagnostic and includes:

  • Device State: Battery level, available RAM, and remaining disk space at the time of a crash.
  • Hardware Specifications: Exact device model and Android operating system version.
  • Crash Context: The specific line of code that triggered the failure and the specific application activity occurring at that moment.

4. Your Rights & Data Deletion

Since your primary content is stored locally, you have total control over it:

  • Manual Deletion: You can delete any event, category, or photo within the app at any time.
  • Full Wipe: Uninstalling the Application will permanently delete all local data and photographs stored on the device.
  • Data Portability: You can use the built-in Backup/Restore feature to export your local data to a ZIP file for your own safekeeping.

5. Children's Privacy

The Application does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and are aware that your child has provided personal information to third-party services via this app, please contact us.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.

7. Contact Us
